## Historical Role And Result

M13-3P adds M10 v2 grouped rigid-body analysis while preserving M10 v1 wire
formats, hashes, FK behavior, collision classification, and clearance
mathematics. `KinematicRigidBody` carries a stable body ID, reference CAD
member, complete member tuple, explicit full-precision offsets, and a semantic
body hash. Every assembly instance belongs to exactly one body; reference
offsets must be identity and others must agree with source placement under
`rigid-transform-agreement@1.0`. V2 joints connect body IDs, and topology is a
deterministic rooted forest with breadth-first traversal and fail-closed
cycle/reachability validation.

`MultiJointKinematicsService` remains the sole FK engine and projects one body
pose to every member. `ExactConstituentPair` is neutral and unordered;
same-body pairs are excluded from generic external measurement but retained at
the inventory boundary. M10-3 reuses the transient exact provider and
`common().Volume`/`distToShape()`. M10-4 keeps the existing conservative proof
and `B_A + B_B` bound, with v2 computing both endpoint bounds. The
implementation includes a post-review trust-boundary repair that reconstructs
and revalidates `KinematicModelV2`, body hashes, schema/evaluator/agreement
versions before FK/provider work.
