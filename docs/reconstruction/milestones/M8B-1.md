## Historical Role And Result

`ProductionApplication.create()` composes StateManager, EvidenceStore,
ownership, ChangeEngine, RunController, ToolRegistry/ToolBroker,
AgentRegistry, ContextBuilder, and AgentGateway. It creates immutable
`ProductionStateBinding` / `ProductionRunBinding`, validates revision/state
hashes and expected sources, enforces fixed trusted identity and exact tool
registration, provides defensive snapshots, and adds narrow per-project
synchronization for revision/run persistence.

It does not add a general workflow executor, CAD ingress, provider bridge,
scheduling, or an OpenCode default.
