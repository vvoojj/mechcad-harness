## Historical Role And Result

M11-5 adds durable, independently reloadable structural Evidence for the
source-bound single-solid linear-static path. Evidence binds project/source
revision/state hash, definition/request, geometry/mesh/solver artifacts,
interpreted results, criteria, material authority, analytical validation, and
trusted provider/parser/execution provenance.

It adds semantic Evidence verification, bounded repeatability, and explicitly
declared three-level mesh convergence. It also corrects distinct C3D10 mesh
realization and same-mesh MSH-to-INP lowering. It does not add M11-6 system
acceptance or general convergence.
