## Limitations

The acceptance does not establish continuous-motion safety, multi-axis or
configuration-space proof, FEA, materials, manufacturing approval, optimization,
or automatic selection. DesignSpec remains a preaccepted caller contract;
compilation provenance is not transitively folded into assembly identity; and
`run_id` is storage/correlation scope only. Recorded assembly/request/result
hashes are historical run values, not replay-stable constants. M10-3 remains a
discrete sweep with `continuous_path_verified = False`; M10-4 proves only one
explicit piecewise-linear joint-space path, not arbitrary configuration-space
regions or general trajectories. The STEP hash is retained audit evidence and
was not independently recomputable from Git because the binary was not
committed.

## Successor Relationship

Evidence, and source immutability foundations while adding continuous and
multi-joint motion analysis. It retains the limitation that ordinary discrete
sweeps are not continuous proof.
